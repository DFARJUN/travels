## Directions — Airport ↔ Hotel

### April 21: Airport → Hotel

**Timeline:**
| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| 13:05 | Land at CPH, Terminal 2B |
| 13:05–13:40 | Immigration + passport control + baggage (Israelis are non-Schengen, expect 30–40 min) |
| ~13:40 | Leave the airport |
| ~14:15 | Arrive at hotel |

**Recommended route — S-tog (commuter rail):**
1. Exit the plane → immigration → baggage → walk to **Terminal 3** platform (adjacent to Terminal 2B)
2. Board the **S-tog** toward **København H** (Central Station)
3. ~14 min ride → exit at **København H**
4. Walk ~7 min to the hotel: exit east side of the station → H. C. Andersens Boulevard → hotel is right on the way
5. **Total: ~25 minutes + walk with luggage**

> **Why S-tog and not Metro M2?**
> The M2 drops you at Kongens Nytorv — from there you'd need another metro (M3) to Rådhuspladsen or a 25-min walk. The S-tog puts you right next to the hotel.

**Ticket:** Buy at the platform machine, zones 1–2, ~36 DKK per person. Or get a City Pass if you plan to use transit heavily.

**Alternative — Bolt/Taxi:**
- ~25–30 min ride
- ~200–280 DKK (~100–130 ILS)
- Only worth it if you're exhausted with heavy luggage late at night

## Transport & Getting Around

**Default rule: always take metro, S-tog, or bus over taxi.** It's faster, cheaper, and Copenhagen's public transit is excellent.

| Mode | Use for | Cost |
|------|---------|------|
| **S-tog** | Airport ↔ city | ~36 DKK |
| **Metro M1/M2/M3** | City center hops | ~24 DKK/ride |
| **Bus** | Nørrebro, Frederiksberg, outer areas | ~24 DKK/ride |
| **Bike (Donkey Republic)** | Any neighborhood exploration | ~30–50 DKK/hour |
| **Bolt/Taxi** | Late night (after ~23:00) or heavy luggage only | ~150–280 DKK |

- **App:** Rejseplanen — for all public transit navigation
- **City Pass:** 24/48/72-hour pass covers metro + bus + S-tog including airport. Worth it if using transit 3+ times a day.
- **Payment:** Denmark is nearly cashless — cards accepted everywhere. Carry a little cash for street markets.
- **Language:** Everyone speaks excellent English.

### Day 2 — April 22 (Full Day): Christianshavn + Christiania + Refshaleøen (SE Day)

> **Weather: 6mm rain - wettest day. SE focus is rain-friendly: Copenhagen Contemporary at Refshaleøen is a massive indoor art space.**

**Logic:** stay SE all day - no zigzag through the city centre.

**Morning (09:00–12:00)**
- Breakfast in Christianshavn - **Lagkagehuset** (Torvegade 45) or **Parterre** (Overgaden Oven Vandet 90)
- **Cross Inderhavnsbroen (Inner Harbour Bridge)** from the Nyhavn side - pedestrian/bike bridge, beautiful entry into the neighbourhood
- **Walk the Christianshavn canals** - Overgaden Oven Vandet + Overgaden Neden Vandet. The neighbourhood's postcard view. Take it slow - you won't come back.
- **Church of Our Saviour** (Vor Frelsers Kirke) - climb the outdoor spiral staircase, stunning views (~60 DKK). Skip if rain is heavy.

**Midday (12:00–14:30)**
- **Freetown Christiania** - the autonomous village. Like nowhere else. Absorb the atmosphere, no photos on Pusher Street
- Lunch in Christianshavn (stays local):
  - **Café Wilder** (Wildersgade 56) - classic Danish bistro, smørrebrød
  - **Morgenstedet** inside Christiania - vegetarian, cheap, fits the detour
  - **Lagkagehuset** Christianshavn - quick casual
  - **Parterre** - canal-side light lunch

**Afternoon (14:30–18:00)**
- Bike (~15 min along the harbour) or bus 2A to **Refshaleøen** - old shipyard-turned-creative-district
- **Copenhagen Contemporary** (Refshalevej 173A) - massive indoor contemporary art space, ~115 DKK. Perfect rain shelter (~1.5 hours).
- **CopenHill / Amager Bakke** (Vindmøllevej 6) - waste-to-energy plant by BIG with a climbable green roof, ski slope, and the world's tallest artificial climbing wall. **Free to walk the roof** - panoramic city views. ~10 min bike south from Copenhagen Contemporary. Iconic, do not skip.
- Walk the Refshaleøen waterfront back - Noma from outside, industrial architecture, harbour views
- Return via **Paper Island (Papirøen) + Inderhavnsbroen** for evening light over the harbour

**Evening (18:00–)**
- Dinner in the SE zone:
  - **Broens Gadekøkken** (Strandgade 95, Paper Island) - covered street food hall on the water, casual, rain-friendly
  - **Christianshavns Færgecafé** (Strandgade 50) - classic smørrebrød + herring + beer, neighbourhood
  - **Barr** (Strandgade 93) - Nordic, former Noma space, pricier

> Reffen and La Banchina also sit on Refshaleøen but their peak is Saturday (Day 5).

### Day 3 — April 23 (Full Day): Juno → Kastellet → Frederiksstaden → Broens → Tivoli

> **Weather: 0.1mm rain — the best day of the trip. Bike + walk outdoor day.**

**Logic:** North-to-south flow. Juno → Kastellet (closest Frederiksstaden-area highlight to Juno) → walk south through Little Mermaid / Nyboder / Marble Church / Amalienborg (12:00 guard) → Broens for lunch → Indre By afternoon → Tivoli for the evening. No backtracking.

**Morning (09:00–10:30) - Juno + Kastellet**
- Rent bikes (Donkey Republic)
- Bike north up Gothersgade to **Juno the Bakery** (Gothersgade 112) - cardamom roll + coffee. Optional pre-stop: **Atelier September** (Gothersgade 30) for sit-down brunch.
- Bike north-east from Juno to **Kastellet** via Sølvgade → Oslo Plads (~10-12 min bike). **Kongens Have / Rosenborg / David Collection stay on Day 4** - don't detour today.
- **Kastellet (The Citadel)** - 17th-century star-shaped fortress. Ramparts walk (~15-min loop), windmill, green lawns. Free.
- *(Optional)* **Resistance Museum (Frihedsmuseet)** - free, right next to Kastellet. Danish WWII history, 30-45 min.

**Midday (11:00–12:30) - Walk South Through Frederiksstaden**
- **Little Mermaid + Gefion Fountain + St. Alban's Church** cluster right south of Kastellet
- **Nyboder** - 1631 yellow naval row houses by Christian IV
- **Marble Church (Marmorkirken / Frederiks Kirke)** - largest dome in Scandinavia. Admire the exterior (free). *(Dome climb opens 13:00 weekdays - doesn't fit this flow, skip today.)*
- **Sankt Annæ Plads + Garrison Church (Garnisons Kirke)** en route
- **12:00 - Amalienborg Palace + Changing of the Guard**

**Lunch (12:45–14:00) - Broens Gadekøkken (Paper Island)**
- Walk/bike south from Amalienborg through Frederiksstaden to **Inderhavnsbroen** (~10 min)
- **Broens Gadekøkken** (Strandgade 95) - covered street food hall on the water. Multiple stalls, split 2-3 between you. Rain-proof.

**Afternoon (14:30–16:00) - Indre By on the Way to Tivoli**
- Cross Inderhavnsbroen back to the city-centre side
- **Nyhavn** - colourful canal postcard, you pass it on the way
- **Christiansborg Tower** - free panoramic city view, last entry 17:00. Moved here from Day 4 - fits the Broens → Tivoli corridor.
- *(Optional)* **Rundetårn** (~40 DKK) or brief **Strøget** walk

**Evening (16:00–) - Tivoli Gardens + Dinner**
- **Tivoli Gardens** - 5 min from the hotel. Entry ~130 DKK, **book online in advance**.
- Dinner inside:
  - **Gemyse** - vegetable-forward Nordic in a greenhouse (book ahead)
  - **Grøften** - classic Danish, old-school Tivoli institution
  - **Mielcke & Hurtigkarl** - upscale, romantic
  - **Gasoline Grill** kiosk - cheap fallback
- Stay until dark - the evening lights are the whole point.

### Day 4 — April 24 (Full Day): Rosenborg + SMK + Designmuseum + Latin Quarter

**Morning (09:00–12:30) — Rosenborg Cluster**
- Breakfast at the hotel or near Rådhuspladsen (Juno + Atelier September were on Day 3)
- **Rosenborg Castle** - 17th-century castle with the Crown Jewels (60–90 min max)
- **Kongens Have** - royal garden surrounding Rosenborg, free, beautiful in spring
- **David Collection** (Kronprinsessegade 30) - free, Islamic art + Danish golden age, tucked into a townhouse, one of Copenhagen's quiet gems. ~45 min. 2-min walk from Kongens Have.
- *(Optional)* **Botanical Garden** - adjacent to Rosenborg, free, Victorian greenhouses
- **Statens Museum for Kunst (SMK)** - Denmark's National Gallery. Free permanent collection - Danish golden age rooms, Matisse, strong modern collection. 10-min walk from Rosenborg. ~1 hour.
- *(Optional)* **Hirschsprung Collection** - right next to SMK, Danish golden age, small and focused

**Midday (12:30–14:30)**
- **Designmuseum Danmark** (Bredgade 68) - one of Europe's best design museums. Danish chairs, Bang & Olufsen, LEGO history, fashion, posters. ~60–90 min. ~120 DKK.
- Smørrebrød lunch at **Hallernes Smørrebrød** in Torvehallerne

**Afternoon + Evening (14:30–)**
- **Latin Quarter** - small streets, independent bookshops, design stores
- **Pisserenden** (Studiestræde + Sankt Peders Stræde) - worth a slow walk
- **Democratic Coffee** - underground cafe, special atmosphere
- Evening: **Nyhavn** for golden hour - best light of the day for photos

### Day 5 — April 25 (Saturday): Nørrebro Market + Frederiksberg

**Morning (09:00–12:00)**

- **Jægersborggade** again — pick up anything you missed earlier
- **Assistens Cemetery** — where H. C. Andersen and Kierkegaard are buried. Danes come here to picnic on the lawns. A strange and genuinely beautiful experience.

**Midday (12:00–15:00)**
- **Reffen** — the waterfront street food market. Saturday is its peak day of the week.
- **La Banchina** (Refshalevej 141, 2-min walk from Reffen) — harbour cafe with natural wine, wood-fired sauna, and people swimming in the harbour. Have a glass of wine on the dock. Nothing else in Copenhagen feels like this.

**Afternoon + Evening (15:00–)**
- **Frederiksberg** - quiet, elegant neighbourhood
- **Frederiksberg Have** - royal gardens with a lake and rowing boats
- *(Optional)* **Studio Arhoj** (Bergmannsgade 6, Vesterbro) - Danish-Japanese ceramics studio. Handmade, very beautiful. Given your love of Japan, this one will hit.
- Farewell dinner in **Nørrebro** (moved from Day 2 - already walking Jægersborggade in the morning, natural to close out here):
  - **Bæst** (Guldbergsgade 29) - Neapolitan pizza with Danish ingredients, wood-fired
  - **Manfreds** (Jægersborggade 40) - organic bistro, great value, small plates

## Practical Tips

- **Book in advance:** Tivoli Gardens (online, skips the queue)
- **Church of Our Saviour:** Check it's open — sometimes closed for events
- **Atelier September:** Arrive by 09:00 before the crowd
- **Reffen:** Closed in winter, opens in April — check current hours before going
- **Christiania:** No photography on Pusher Street
- **Bikes:** Donkey Republic app, ~30–50 DKK/hour — Copenhagen is built for cycling
- **April:** 10–11°C — layers + waterproof jacket. Can get sunny and beautiful.
- **Cards everywhere:** Denmark is Europe's most cashless country. Visa/Mastercard accepted universally.
- **Language:** Everyone speaks excellent English.
- **Transit over taxi:** Always take metro/S-tog/bus. Taxi only for late night or emergencies.
