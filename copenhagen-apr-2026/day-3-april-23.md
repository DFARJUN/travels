# Day 3 - April 23 (Thursday): Juno → Kastellet → Frederiksstaden → Broens → Tivoli

> **Weather: 11°C high, 6°C low, 0.1mm rain - the best day of the trip. Bike + walk outdoor day.**

**Shape:** Bike north to Juno for breakfast. Continue north-east to Kastellet (closest Frederiksstaden-area highlight to Juno). Walk south through the Little Mermaid / Gefion / St. Alban's cluster, then into Nyboder + Marble Church + Sankt Annæ Plads + Garrison Church, arriving at Amalienborg for the 12:00 changing of the guard. South to Broens Gadekøkken for lunch. Afternoon in Indre By (Christiansborg Tower) en route to Tivoli. Evening + dinner at Tivoli.

**Flow logic:** north-to-south. Every stop is downstream of the previous one - no backtracking.

---

## Morning (09:00–10:30) — Juno + Kastellet

> **Note:** Kongens Have, Rosenborg, David Collection, Botanical Garden = Day 4 cluster. Don't detour there today even though they're near Juno.

- **09:00 - Rent bikes** (Donkey Republic, pick up near the hotel)
- **09:15 - Bike north up Gothersgade to Juno the Bakery** (Gothersgade 112) - ~10 min bike
  - **Cardamom roll** is the legendary order. Grab one plus a coffee.
  - Optional stop en route: **Atelier September** (Gothersgade 30) for a sit-down brunch before Juno
- **09:45 - Bike north-east to Kastellet** - Sølvgade → Oslo Plads → Kastellet main entrance (~10-12 min bike). Kastellet is the closest major Frederiksstaden-area highlight to Juno.
- **10:00 - Kastellet (The Citadel)** - 17th-century star-shaped fortress. Walk the **ramparts (~15-min loop)**, see the windmill, take in the quiet green lawns. Free.
- **10:30 - Optional: Resistance Museum (Frihedsmuseet)** - free, right next to Kastellet. Danish WWII resistance history, 30-45 min. Skip if you want to move faster.

---

## Midday (11:00–12:30) — Walk South Through Frederiksstaden

North-to-south walk ending at Amalienborg for the 12:00 guard ceremony.

- **11:00 - Little Mermaid + Gefion + St. Alban's cluster** (all 2-3 min apart, right south of Kastellet):
  - **The Little Mermaid (Den Lille Havfrue)** - the icon, smaller than expected
  - **Gefion Fountain** - dramatic Norse-goddess sculpture, major photo spot
  - **St. Alban's Church (Den engelske kirke)** - English Anglican, neo-Gothic, deeply photogenic
- **11:20 - Walk/bike south-west to Nyboder** (Skt. Pauls Plads) - 1631 yellow naval row houses by Christian IV
- **11:35 - Marble Church (Marmorkirken / Frederiks Kirke)** - largest dome in Scandinavia. Admire the exterior (free). *(The dome climb opens at 13:00 weekdays - doesn't fit this flow, skip it today.)*
- **11:45 - Sankt Annæ Plads + Garrison Church (Garnisons Kirke)** - elegant tree-lined square + 17th-century military church
- **12:00 - Amalienborg Palace + Changing of the Guard** - rococo royal residence, four palaces around an octagonal square

---

## Lunch (12:45–14:00) — Broens Gadekøkken (Paper Island)

- **12:30 - Walk/bike south from Amalienborg through Frederiksstaden** toward the harbour → **Inderhavnsbroen** (Inner Harbour Bridge). ~10 min.
- **Broens Gadekøkken** (Strandgade 95, Paper Island) - covered street food hall on the water
  - Multiple stalls, casual, waterfront tables
  - Pick 2-3 stalls between you and split
  - Covered, so rain-proof if weather changes

---

## Afternoon (14:30–16:00) — Indre By on the Way to Tivoli

From Broens back toward Tivoli - walk or bike across Inderhavnsbroen to the city-centre side.

- **Nyhavn** - the colourful canal postcard. You pass it on the way back.
- **Christiansborg Tower** - climb for a panoramic city view, **completely free**. Last entry 17:00 - this afternoon window is the right moment. 5-min walk from Nyhavn.
- *(Optional instead)* **Rundetårn (Round Tower)** (Købmagergade 52A) - 17th-century spiral-ramp tower, ~40 DKK, 10 min from Nyhavn
- *(Optional)* brief **Strøget** walk on the way to the hotel / Tivoli

> Christiansborg Tower is the strongest call here - free, dramatic views, and it's on the route. Originally on Day 4 but a better fit here.

---

## Evening (16:00–) — Tivoli Gardens + Dinner

- **16:00 - Tivoli Gardens** - 5-min walk from Rådhuspladsen / the hotel
- Entry ~130 DKK. **Book online in advance** to skip the queue.
- Rides, gardens, live music, beer halls, Tivoli Concert Hall

**Dinner inside Tivoli:**
- **Gemyse** - vegetable-forward Nordic in a beautiful greenhouse. Book ahead, popular.
- **Grøften** - classic Danish, old-school Tivoli institution, smørrebrød + fish + beer
- **Mielcke & Hurtigkarl** - upscale, romantic
- **Gasoline Grill** kiosk - cheap burger fallback
- Or just snack through the gardens

**Stay until dark** - the evening lighting is the whole point.
