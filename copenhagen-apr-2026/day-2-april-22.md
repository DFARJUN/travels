# Day 2 - April 22 (Wednesday): Christianshavn + Christiania + Refshaleøen (SE Day)

> **Weather: 11°C high, 6°C low, 6mm rain - wettest day of the trip. SE focus is rain-friendly: Copenhagen Contemporary at Refshaleøen is a massive indoor art space. Waterproof jacket + layers.**

**Logic:** stay in the SE quadrant all day - no backtracking to the north. Christianshavn, Christiania, and Refshaleøen all connect by a short bike or bus ride along the harbour.

---

## Morning (09:00–12:00) — Breakfast + Christianshavn

- Breakfast in Christianshavn (local, no northbound detour)
  - **Lagkagehuset** (Torvegade 45) - Danish bakery chain, solid pastries + coffee, fast
  - **Parterre** (Overgaden Oven Vandet 90) - canal-side cafe, sit-down breakfast
- **Walk to Christianshavn via Inderhavnsbroen** (Inner Harbour Bridge) - cross at Nyhavn / Paper Island. Pedestrian + bike bridge, beautiful entry into the neighbourhood. ~15 min from the hotel via Nyhavn.
- **Walk the Christianshavn canals** - Overgaden Oven Vandet + Overgaden Neden Vandet. Colourful merchant houses, moored boats, the neighbourhood's postcard view. Take this slow - you won't come back.
- **Church of Our Saviour** (Vor Frelsers Kirke)
  - Climb the outdoor spiral staircase (~60 DKK)
  - Check it's open before going - sometimes closed for events
  - Rain note: staircase is exposed. If rain is heavy, skip and do it on a drier day

---

## Midday (12:00–14:30) — Christiania + Lunch in Christianshavn

- **Freetown Christiania** - the autonomous village. Like nowhere else.
  - Absorb the atmosphere. **No photos on Pusher Street.**
- Lunch in Christianshavn - stays local
  - **Café Wilder** (Wildersgade 56) - classic Danish bistro, smørrebrød, ~120-180 DKK
  - **Morgenstedet** (inside Christiania) - vegetarian, cheap, fits the Christiania flow
  - **Lagkagehuset** Christianshavn - quick sandwich + pastry
  - **Parterre** (Overgaden Oven Vandet 90) - canal-side light lunch

---

## Afternoon (14:30–18:00) — Refshaleøen

- Bike (~15 min along the harbour) or bus 2A from Christianshavn to **Refshaleøen** - old shipyard-turned-creative-district
- **Copenhagen Contemporary** (Refshalevej 173A) - massive indoor contemporary art space. ~115 DKK. Perfect rain shelter - budget 1.5 hours.
- **CopenHill / Amager Bakke** (Vindmøllevej 6) - the icon. A working waste-to-energy plant with a climbable green roof, ski slope, and the tallest artificial climbing wall in the world. Designed by BIG (Bjarke Ingels). **Free to walk the roof** for panoramic city views. ~10 min bike south from Copenhagen Contemporary. Do not skip this - it's one of the defining pieces of modern Copenhagen architecture.
- Walk the Refshaleøen waterfront on the way back - Noma from outside, industrial architecture, harbour views
- **Return via Paper Island (Papirøen) + Inderhavnsbroen** for the evening light over the harbour

> **Reffen and La Banchina** also sit on Refshaleøen, but their peak is Saturday. Save them for Day 5.

---

## Evening (18:00–) — Dinner in the SE Zone

Pick one:
- **Broens Gadekøkken** (Strandgade 95, Paper Island / Inderhavnsbroen) - covered street food hall on the water. Casual, many stalls, rain-friendly.
- **Christianshavns Færgecafé** (Strandgade 50) - classic smørrebrød, herring, beer, neighbourhood feel
- **Barr** (Strandgade 93, next to Broens Gadekøkken) - former Noma space, Nordic, pricier but excellent
