## Day by Day

### Day 0 — Thu 17.9 — Arrival
- Land late night. Pick up rental car (check the desk is open; some close at midnight — if the flight is very late, sleep near the airport and collect the car in the morning).
- Sleep near Catania airport. Don't plan anything.

### Day 1 — Fri 18.9 — Catania street food → Ortigia
- **Morning:** La Pescheria fish market behind the Duomo. Arancini + pane e panelle. Coffee at Prestipino. Piazza del Duomo (black lava elephant fountain).
- **Midday:** Via Etnea stroll, granita with brioche.
- **Afternoon:** Drive to Ortigia (~1 hr), settle in for 3 nights.
- **Evening:** Sunset and dinner in Ortigia's alleys.

### Day 2 — Sat 19.9 — Greek Syracuse + Noto
- **Morning:** Parco Archeologico della Neapolis — Greek theater, Ear of Dionysius. Go early before the heat.
- **Midday:** Ortigia morning market, monster sandwich at Caseificio Borderi. Piazza Duomo, Fountain of Arethusa.
- **Afternoon:** Noto — Sicilian baroque, golden stone. Gelato at Caffè Sicilia.
- **Evening:** Marzamemi fishing village (or Calamosche beach if you want sea).

### Day 3 — Sun 20.9 — Valley of the Temples, Agrigento
- **Morning:** Pack up, drive west to Agrigento (~2.5-3 hr, the longest leg).
- **Afternoon/Evening:** Valle dei Templi — Greek temples on a ridge above the sea, better preserved than in Greece itself. Walk at golden hour (cooler + best light). Some temples lit at night.
- Sleep in Agrigento. Simple local dinner. One-night stopover.

### Day 4 — Mon 21.9 — Palermo, the wonderful chaos
- **Morning:** Drive to Palermo (~2 hr), settle in for 3 nights.
- **Midday:** Ballarò or Vucciria market. Real street food: panelle, sfincione, stigghiola, and pani ca meusa (spleen sandwich) if you're brave.
- **Afternoon:** Quattro Canti, Fontana Pretoria, the Cathedral, Teatro Massimo.
- **Evening:** Aperitivo in Vucciria (turns into a street party at night).

### Day 5 — Tue 22.9 — Monreale + deep Palermo
- **Morning:** Monreale Cathedral (20 min from Palermo) — Byzantine gold mosaics, among the most beautiful anywhere.
- **Midday:** Il Capo market.
- **Afternoon:** Cappella Palatina in the Norman Palace (more gold mosaics), or wander the Kalsa quarter.
- **Evening:** Gelato and fresh cannoli, casual local dinner.

### Day 6 — Wed 23.9 — Mondello + free Palermo
- **Morning:** Mondello beach — turquoise water, art-nouveau villas, 20 min from center.
- **Midday:** Fish or pasta at Mondello.
- **Afternoon:** Last markets, shopping, anything you missed.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner in Palermo.

### Day 7 — Thu 24.9 — Cefalù en route → Taormina
- **Morning:** Drive east, stop in Cefalù (1 hr from Palermo).
- **Midday:** Old town, Norman cathedral, beach and lunch. Optional 20-30 min climb up La Rocca for the view.
- **Afternoon:** Continue to Taormina (~2.5 hr), settle in for 2 nights.
- **Evening:** Piazza IX Aprile, sunset and sea view.
- **Note:** ~4 hr total driving, broken by Cefalù. If you want to soften it, swap a Palermo night for a Cefalù night.

### Day 8 — Fri 25.9 — Mount Etna + Taormina
- **Morning:** Etna — cable car (Funivia from Rifugio Sapienza) or the lower lava trails (Crateri Silvestri, free, impressive).
- **Midday:** Wine and cheese at a small winery on the slopes — a genuinely one-of-a-kind culinary stop.
- **Afternoon:** Teatro Antico di Taormina (Greek theater with Etna + sea view), Corso Umberto, granita at Bam Bar.
- **Evening:** Isola Bella or the hilltop village Castelmola. Last night.

### Day 9 — Sat 26.9 — Fly from Catania
- **Morning:** Short drive to Catania airport (~50 min).
- Return car, fly home.

## Practical Notes

- **ZTL zones:** Every old town (Catania, Ortigia, Taormina, Cefalù, Palermo) has restricted-traffic areas. Park outside and walk in.
- **One-way car rental:** Confirm same-airport pickup/return to avoid a one-way fee (it's the same airport here, so fine).
- **Longest drives:** Ortigia → Agrigento (~2.5-3 hr) and Palermo → Taormina (~4 hr with the Cefalù stop). Two long-ish driving days out of nine.
- **Alternative:** Swap one Palermo night for a Cefalù night to break the westbound drive better (trade-off: less time in Palermo).
